# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Linux players using a keyboard, mouse, or controller at a desk or from the couch. They need one dependable place to launch Steam, Epic, manual, and emulated games without losing controller support or library context.

## Product Purpose

Nexus Play is a unified Linux game launcher. It should make finding, launching, managing, and returning from a game feel immediate and reliable across PC and console-style use.

## Brand Personality

Focused, tactile, confident.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic dashboard chrome, high-chroma neon, busy store-like surfaces, and interfaces that require a mouse for primary game-launch flows.

## Design Principles

- Let game artwork and library state lead the screen.
- Make launch, stop, and controller state unambiguous.
- Keep keyboard and controller paths equivalent.
- Prefer a dense, calm tool surface over decorative cards.
- Preserve console-style immersion in Big Picture mode.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain keyboard and controller parity, visible focus states, high-contrast text and states, and reduced-motion fallbacks.
